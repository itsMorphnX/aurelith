Suggestions & Bugs

Found a bug, hit a crash, or have an idea for the book? Tell us here. Every report goes straight to the team's channel.

What is it?

Good reports get fixed first

  • One problem per report. Two bugs? Send two.
  • Say where. Which mission, room or menu, and what you were doing.
  • Crashes: in the game, open the menu and press Report a bug. It attaches the game's log for you.
  • Ideas welcome. Big or small: a spell, a crew line, a cosy corner of the capital.
Reports are read by the team. Please don't include passwords or anything private.
