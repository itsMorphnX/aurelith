The Codex Night

Receive your grimoire

Make your apprentice, face the Soul Trial and watch your book bind itself: its cover, its element, its first spell and the paper familiar folded from its pages.

Fight together

Steel, spells and a crew

Swords, spears, bows, staves and gauntlets, each with its own combos. Mix elements for reactions, read the tells and roll. Friends drop in and take a crew member's place.

The Royal Capital

Craft, trade and rest

Forge and upgrade gear, brew potions, carve runes, cook party meals and dye your cloak. Every station levels up as you use it.
